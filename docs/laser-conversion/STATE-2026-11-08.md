## 4. Measurements and open electrical question

### 4.1 PA7 (established)

| Observation | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| HIGH ≈ 3.3 V, LOW ≈ 1.6 V | Divider with pull-up through series R — **not** clean 0 V |
| ~844 mVpp curved @ ~1 kHz | **C13** filter — firmware cannot remove |
| Conclusion | Z header **abandoned** for direct laser control |

### 4.2 PA0 / PA2 (unresolved)

User transcription (images unavailable at time; endpoints not confirmed):

- Repeating ~1 kHz curved small modulation; duty changes with menu on fan **and** bed  
- Remains **near 24 V**, no full swing  
- Known 24 V fan connected and **spinning**  
- Scope PM3082 1:1; AC then DC; 20 mV/div → cursor ΔV ~50–58 mV; 0.5 ms/div; **5 V/div flatline**  
- Meter “24 V on those wires” — exact endpoints unconfirmed  
- Cursor deltas are **not** automatically period or Vpp  

**No confirmed cause for “no swing.”** Do not blame the user. Do not claim SCOPE9 firmware fixed hardware. First next step is **static GPIO OFF/ON** with tip on switched − and ground on DC− (see GETTING-STARTED).

### 4.3 Expected static readings (under known 24 V load — not agent-verified)

| Where | GPIO OFF | GPIO ON |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tip on FAN−/BED− vs PSU− | ≈ **24 V** | ≈ **0 V** |
| Across load (+ to −) | ≈ **0 V** | ≈ **24 V** |

Scope ground **only** on board DC−. Never on FAN−/BED−. DC coupling; 24 V–rated probe/load.

## 5. SCOPE9 audit — D1–D5 (bugs vs diagnostic improvements)

Full write-up: [`SCOPE9-POWER-DIAG.md`](SCOPE9-POWER-DIAG.md).

| ID | Class | Finding | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **D1** | **Confirmed defect** | AllOFF parked PA0/PA2 only; PA7 Scope could remain live | AllOFF / `fan_bench_stop` parks **PA0+PA2+PA7** |
| **D2** | **Diagnostic gap** (fixed) | Fan/Bed menus were PWM-only — could not separate timer/AF from MOSFET path | Persistent **GPIO OFF/ON** on all three targets |
| **D3** | **Confirmed defect** | Split Scope vs FanBench menu allowlists cancelled PA7 when opening Fan/Bed submenus inconsistently | Unified allowlist for Laser Control + three submenus |
| **D4** | **Confirmed defect / hygiene** | `SET_PWM` (= `pinMode(OUTPUT)`) before `set_pwm_duty` briefly forced GPIO; park remux awkward | PWM via `set_pwm_duty`→`pinmap_pinout` only; park = duty0 then GPIO LOW |
| **D5** | **Confirmed defect** | Soft-PWM ISR could fight FanPA0 **GPIO HIGH** every TEMP tick | Soft-PWM skipped whenever `power_diag_owns_fan0()` |

**Not established:** any of D1–D5 as the root cause of the ~50 mV near-24 V observation.

**Honest negative:** PinMap / TEMP→TIM7 / bed-pin disable audit found **no** source defect that would force drains to stay near 24 V while only emitting tens of mV of PWM if tip/ground are correct.

Engineering note (not article quote): a brushless fan can hold charge between PWM pulses and muddy OFF rise. A **defined resistive load across output + and switched −** is a **pull-UP/load for the drain**, not a pull-down. Example only (not performed): **2.2 kΩ 1 W** @ 24 V ≈ 10.9 mA / 0.26 W — switching visibility test, not current-capacity test.
